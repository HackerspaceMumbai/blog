Three developer ecosystems. One Saturday in Mumbai.

Join **#Nov26MTP** on **Saturday, 28 November 2026** at **Microsoft Mumbai** for **GitHub Universe ’26 Recap × .NET Conf 2026 Local × Docker Meetup | Mumbai**. Organized by **Hackerspace Mumbai**, this in-person gathering is part of **Mumbai Technology Meetup (#mumtechup)** and supported by **Docker** and **CodeRabbit**.

## One gathering for builders

- **GitHub Universe ’26 Recap:** Explore the announcements, AI-powered developer experiences, tools, and ideas from GitHub Universe, with a focus on what we can actually build.
- **.NET Conf 2026 Local:** Bring .NET Conf home to Mumbai with conversations and demos around modern .NET, AI, Aspire, Blazor, developer tooling, and real-world application development.
- **Docker Meetup:** Our first Docker meetup in Mumbai under Docker's new identity. Explore containers, Run on Trust, Docker Sandboxes, Docker AI Governance, and trusted execution for AI agents.

Expect practical talks and live demos rather than keynote replays, open-source projects and experiments, lessons learned, and plenty of time to meet other builders. A hands-on workshop may be added if the right community session fits the programme; any laptop or setup requirements will be shared with registered attendees beforehand.

## Have something to share?

The **#mumtechup call for proposals is open** for lightning talks, technical talks and live demos, and a possible hands-on workshop. First-time speakers are welcome. Side projects, open-source work, capstone projects, experiments, research, lessons learned, and useful failures all count.
